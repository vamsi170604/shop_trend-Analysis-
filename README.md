## 📊 Shop Trend Analytics: Retail Sales & Customer Insights Dashboard

An interactive and data-driven Power BI dashboard designed to analyze retail sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, product trends, profitability, and regional business performance through dynamic visualizations and KPI monitoring.

# Purpose of Dashboard
The Shop Trend Analytics Dashboard is a comprehensive Business Intelligence project developed to monitor and analyze key retail business metrics such as revenue generation, profit performance, customer behavior, product demand, regional sales contribution, and discount impact.
The dashboard transforms raw retail transaction data into actionable business insights that help businesses optimize sales strategies, improve profitability, and make data-driven decisions.

# Tech Stack
The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:
- 📊 Power BI – Main platform used for dashboard development and visualization.
- 📂 Power Query – Used for data cleaning, transformation, and preprocessing.
- 📑 DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – Used for KPI calculations, measures, and business metrics.
- 🗄️ SQL – Used for querying retail sales data and generating business insights.
- 📈 Data Modeling – Relationships created among sales, customers, products, and regional data.
- 📁 File Formats – .pbix for Power BI dashboard files and .png for dashboard previews.

# Data Source
Source:<br> 
Retail shopping trends dataset containing customer transactions, product purchases, sales performance, customer demographics, payment information, ratings, and regional sales data.<br>

The dataset includes:
- Customer Information
- Product Categories
- Product Details
- Sales Revenue
- Profit & Profit Margin
- Quantity Sold
- Customer Ratings
- Discounts
- Payment Methods
- Subscription Status
- Regional & City Performance
- Monthly Sales Trends<br>

The data was cleaned, transformed, and modeled using Power Query and Power BI.

 # Highlights of Dashboard
Business Problem : <br>

Retail businesses often struggle to identify:<br>
- Which products generate the highest revenue?
- Which categories are most profitable?
- Which customers contribute the most revenue?
- Which cities and regions drive business growth?
- How do discounts impact profitability?
- Which products receive the highest customer ratings?
- Which subscription groups generate more revenue?

Answering these questions through raw transactional data is difficult and time-consuming.

The primary goals of this dashboard are to:<br>

Provide a centralized view of retail business performance.
- Track revenue and profitability KPIs.
- Analyze customer purchasing behavior.
- Evaluate product and category performance.
- Compare regional and city-level performance.
- Measure discount effectiveness.
- Support strategic business decisions using data visualization.

- Total Revenue
- Total Profit

These KPIs provide a quick overview of overall business performance.

This visual compares revenue generated across product categories.
It helps businesses identify:
- High-performing categories
- Revenue-driving products

Displays category-wise profit contribution.

➜  Customer Contribution Analysis (Donut / Bar Chart)<br>
